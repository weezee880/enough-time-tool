This tool helps you work out how much genuinely free time you have in a typical week, and whether that's likely to be enough to study this module alongside everything else you're juggling. It takes about two minutes. The result is a guide, not a verdict — only you know your own week.

What this means for you

Results are a guide only. Actual study time varies week to week — expect to need more during assignment periods and less in quieter weeks. If you're weighing this up, it's worth talking it through with the module team before you enrol.
